# PowerMonitor

In January 2026, I finally  got tired of windows, and decided to switch my laptop to linux. I have been running linux for years, but mainly on my desktop, and servers at work. I think I tried it once 15 years ago.

But windows is _really_ bad. 

So, I bit the bullet and installed fedora. Luckily my machine is a framework, which ostensibly has good linux support, and linux driver support for laptops has come a long way in 15 years.

After a fun adventure getting hibernate + full disk-encryption setup. (see here) I had a nice snappy windows experience.

But then I run into the sad reality that my battery life sucks. It is not horrible, giving me 4.5 hours, at minimal usage.
